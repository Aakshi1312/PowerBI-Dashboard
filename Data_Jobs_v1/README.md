# DATA JOBS DASHBOARD

![alt text](/images/data%20dashboard.jpg)

## INTRODUCTION

Navigating the data job marketcan feel like a maze with information scattered everywhere. This dashboard (V2.0) is designed specially for **Job seekers, Job transitioners and Job Swappers** to cut thorugh the cutter! Using a real word dataset of 2024data science job postings rich with details on titles, salaries and locations-this project offer a streamlined, single page interface to quickly explore crucial market trends andcompensation insights

## Skills Showcased

- **Dashboard Design:** Crafting a intutitive and visually appealing report layout.

- **Power Querry ETL:** Performing data cleaning, shapping and transformation.

- **Data Modelling :** Building efficient data model with relationship(Star schema priciples)

- **DAX Fundamental** : Creating calculation and aggregationsto derive key insights

- **Visualization Utilizeed:**
  - **Core Charts :** Column,Bar,Area charts to comparisons and trends
  - **Map Charts:** For displaying geospatical data
  - **Cards :**To highlight key performance indicators.
  - **Tables:** For representing detail and tabular information.
  - **Chart Variety:**Selecting from common and uncommon chart type for effective storytelling.
- **Interactive Features:**
  - **Slicing :**Enabling dynamic,user driven data filtering
  - **Button Bookmarks:** For streamlined navigationand managingreport view (including drill through)

## Dashboard Preview

The second iteration consolidates the dashboard into a **single, focused page**, designed to give job seekers the most critical job insight at their fingertips

![alt text](/images/project_2.png)

This page acts as your concise mission control for the data job market . It showcases key performance indicators(KPIs) like **job count, skill per job ,Median Yearly Salary, and Median Hourly Salary** .We can also quickly see **Skill Popularity** (by job percent or count) and compare **Salries across differnt job title**,all designed for a efficient overview.

## Conclusions:

This updated dashboard (Version 2.0) showcases Power BI (2.0) ability to transform extensive job posting data into powerful, streamlined tool for carrer analysis. It empower **Job Seekers , Job transitioner and Job Swappers** by allowing them to filter and explore essential market insights effeciently on a single page, helping them make informed decision about their next career move.
